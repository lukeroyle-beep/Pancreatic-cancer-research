# Ninety-day research roadmap

The 2 October 2026 HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee table confirms that none of the seven absent genes can be recovered by an approved, previous or alias symbol in the external expression matrix. The exact 130-gene test is blocked with that input. First seek a permitted complete matrix for the same models and reproduce the original score in development data. Any 123-gene adaptation needs explicit owner approval and a separately locked estimand. [Input verification](../datasets/pharmacotyping.md).

27 November 2026 to 24 February 2027 inclusive. Dates are planning windows, not commitments to make a discovery. The human owner decides material changes and external publication. The six decisions below implement the brief while retaining the four broader milestones in the [foundation protocol](README.md).

**Mapping to existing governance.** Foundation milestone 1 covers the new Gates 1–2 (gap and data). Milestone 2 covers Gate 3 (reproduction). Milestone 3 is the preregistration required before Gate 4. Milestone 4 covers Gates 5–6 (independent validation and specialist review). This mapping adds acceptance criteria and does not supersede repository instructions.

**Possible external review, subject to owner approval.** A pancreatic oncology and organoid specialist can assess the clinical meaning of an assay endpoint and whether a validation cohort is genuinely separate. A statistical reviewer can challenge the estimand, leakage and power. An RNA thermodynamics specialist can test equivalence of competitors and energy settings. The University of Birmingham, Pancreatic Cancer UK, Cancer Research UK, the Institute of Cancer Research or the Francis Crick Institute are possible future points of review; no affiliation, endorsement, availability or contact is implied.
