# PCR-005 | What transfers from inverse-folding theory?

**Evidence cut-off: 27 November 2026.** This assessment covers single-strand ribonucleic acid (RNA) secondary-structure inverse folding. The practical question is whether a certificate or ranking in one model survives a stated change of model. The programme has no evidence that a certificate predicts pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) treatment response. The [owner asset register](assets.md) audits the separate pre-existing mathematical work. The [search record](rna-search.md) and [screening ledger](rna-screening.tsv) give sources and retrieval limitations.

## Claims and supersession

| Claim under consideration | What the evidence covers | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Linear-time inverse folding in a base-pair count model | Boury, Gardelle, Bulteau and Ponty, 24 October 2025, prove that for a noncrossing target whose shortest maximal helix contains at least three base pairs, designability is equivalent to avoiding two local motifs and to admitting a proper colouring separated modulo two. The competing structures allow Watson–Crick pairs and G–U wobble. Separation distinguishes the residue classes of leaves and grey nodes on a level-labelled tree. A design has the target as its **unique** optimum. Corollaries 1–2 give linear time and space on this class. With arbitrary modulus the stated algorithm takes time proportional to n·m·2ᵐ and space proportional to n·m. | Published mathematical proof. The 2024 conference paper is the precursor, not independent confirmation. This review did not formally recheck the proof. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-025-00278-6) · [Conference predecessor](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2024.19) |
| Turner energy performance in the 2025 study | Figure 15 compares 1,000 synthetic targets of length 100. Each strategy contributes one sample per target. The minimum loop-spacing parameter is three here (the article’s formal default is zero). In the Turner 1999 parameterisation, positive energy distance was reported for 10%, 35% and 43% of compatible, separated and relaxed seeds, respectively. The endpoint considers alternatives at base-pair distance at least three and limits the suboptimal-energy search to a 5 kcal/mol window. A tied or better neighbour at distance one or two is outside that endpoint. The comparison to INFO-RNA concerns initialisation. | Published computational experiment. “35% have a uniquely correct fold under Turner” exceeds this endpoint. Synthetic, restricted targets and one draw per method limit generalisation. [Article, Figure 15 and metric definition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12553252/) |
| First exact extension from counting base pairs to counting stacks | Boury, Bulteau and Ponty, 27 August 2026, already prove a linear-time result for unweighted stacked-base-pair maximisation. In the noncrossing setting a sufficient lower bound on minimum helix length is ⌈log₃.₅₆(Δ) + 6.2⌉, where Δ is the largest loop degree. A separate theorem allowing crossing pairs uses the number of helices in place of Δ. The sufficient criterion is called *locked*. It is distinct from modulo-separability. Section 7 claims that the construction also solves weighted stack models when non-wobble stacks are more stabilising than wobble-containing stacks. The implication for the owner’s competitor set and numerical table has not been independently established here. | Direct prior art. Any weighted extension requires comparison of exact sequence classes, alternative folds, numerical inequalities and parameters. A larger count alone cannot order arbitrary sums of heterogeneous weights. No unrestricted Turner theorem was located. [Proceedings and publication date](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.12) · [Section 7 and proofs](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol390-wabi2026/html/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.12/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.12.html) |
| First evaluation of stack designs in a Turner model | The same 2026 paper compares Locked⁺ with random compatible assignments on 1,960 synthetic target draws. Section 5 evaluates uniqueness and the best distinct alternative using a 5 kcal/mol suboptimal-energy window. It reports 73% and 45% Turner designs overall for the respective methods. These are the authors' rounded rates. The 2025 study's distance-three endpoint measures something different. | Already tested. A replication must preserve the competing structure set, energy settings and censoring. Re-tabulation is a feasible audit of the same data, not independent biological validation. [Section 5](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol390-wabi2026/html/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.12/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.12.html) · [Versioned data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20266458) |
| Other formal and ensemble results | Jogalekar's 25 August 2026 **preprint** reports designability with at most two length-two maximal helices, no length-one helix and all remaining helices at least three pairs long, within a four-letter Watson–Crick-only base-pair count model, zero minimum span and the stated motif exclusions. Its reported Lean proof was not rerun. Zhou et al. give rival-based undesignability criteria for thermodynamic motifs and subsequently bound achievable target probability on ArchiveII and Eterna100. These are respectively impossibility or probability results, not guarantees of a constructive Turner design for every otherwise eligible target. | These results constrain claims of originality. Mark a new weighted family or ensemble study provisional until an independent reviewer compares hypotheses and coverage. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25194) · [Rival-motif analysis](https://doi.org/10.1177/15578666251398551) · [Ensemble bounds](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag246) |

Stack count, nearest-neighbour stack energies and a full Turner model define different objectives. Full Turner scoring also includes loop initiation and sequence-dependent loop/end terms. A fixed positive margin in one model supplies no general lower bound on the full-model margin without bounding the added energy differences for **all** allowable competitors. A single artificial family also cannot support a claim about all RNA structures. The owner repository's documented three-helix, Watson–Crick-only weighted result is therefore relevant prior programme work. Its theorem statement, support and reuse constraints belong in the [asset register](assets.md). [Nearest Neighbor Database](https://rna.urmc.rochester.edu/NNDB/)

## Proposed validation and scientific interpretation

1. **Reproduce the existing evidence.** Lock a protocol before inspecting outcome distributions. First reconstruct the reported synthetic-target proportions and their denominators from the archived columns, using the two outcomes as paired observations of the same draw. Preserve the paper's target-draw estimand. Add a distinct-structure-weighted sensitivity analysis and uncertainty grouped by identical structure. Document whether repeated pseudorandom seeds create dependence across parameter cells. Check thresholds, sign, ties, censoring and exact settings against the archive. A disagreement is a result to retain.
2. **Test robustness without changing the question.** Positive controls should include an analytically unambiguous eligible artificial structure under the declared model. Negative controls should include a certified tie and a prohibited motif under the corresponding base-pair model. Record method failures and timeouts as outcomes. Compare the authors' compatible baseline, the published algorithm and, where provenance permits, an established solver such as INFO-RNA or RNAiFold. Seed-only and end-to-end comparisons need separate endpoints and fixed computational budgets. [INFO-RNA](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btl194) · [RNAiFold 2.0](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkv460)
3. **Test ensemble conclusions separately.** Target probability is the equilibrium probability assigned to the complete target. Normalised ensemble defect is the expected fraction of positions with incorrect pairing state or partner. A reported minimum-free-energy (MFE) structure can tie with other structures or have low ensemble occupancy. A second evaluator should use the same free-energy parameter set, temperature, dangling ends, wobble and loop conventions. Distinguish independent software verification on identical inputs from validation on disjoint, preregistered artificial motifs. Report paired effect sizes and uncertainty intervals, a single primary endpoint, and multiplicity-adjusted exploratory strata. [Parameter reference](https://rna.urmc.rochester.edu/NNDB/) · [Prior ensemble designability analysis](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag246)
4. **Therapeutic relevance is indirect.** A model-transfer audit could identify when predicted structure is too uncertain to support downstream prioritisation. The phase I PDAC RNA neoantigen vaccine study and its follow-up establish an RNA modality of clinical interest. Neither tested this mathematical approach. Delivery, chemical modifications, intracellular binding and folding, translation, biological activity and clinical outcomes require their own evidence. Structural probing and biophysical comparison would be necessary to assess a structural claim experimentally. Any subsequent work on PDAC material would need a defined independent biological rationale, specialist review and the owner's approval. [Phase I study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06063-y) · [Follow-up](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08508-4)

**Confidence.** High for the scope of the two peer-reviewed Boury results, the archival identifiers and the file integrity counts. Moderate for prioritising a paired reanalysis, subject to an independent prior-art search and review of the archive's scoring implementation. Low for clinical relevance beyond methodological plausibility. No new therapeutic result, general weighted/Turner theorem or external biological validation has been established.
