### A. Does a published gemcitabine score retain its ordering between organoid laboratories?

**Known and competing work.** Tiriac and colleagues published gemcitabine pharmacotyping and transcriptomic signatures and studied clinical cohorts. Other investigators developed GemPred, evaluated different transcriptomic scores, profiled combination therapy and related organoid findings to patient outcomes. An independent validation of *a* gemcitabine score, or the claim that organoids differ in sensitivity, already has substantial precedent. [Tiriac 2018](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-18-0349) · [Nicolle](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2020.10.601) · [PancreasView](https://doi.org/10.1093/oncolo/oyaf083).

**Unresolved question and data.** For a specified version of Tiriac's original gemcitabine score, does higher predicted sensitivity rank independently derived, histology-confirmed organoids by *measured single-agent* sensitivity at a second laboratory? The proposed test is Shi and colleagues' organoids, conditional on reconstructing score normalisation, sample identity, dose range, timing, response direction and a donor-disjoint set with usable expression. In a response-masked audit all 39 assayed models joined to expression and pathology, including 38 ductal organoids, each recorded as one sample per patient. The source gene list has 130 eligible members and the initial exact-symbol match recovered 123. Across these two cohorts the assay duration (five versus three days) is confounded with laboratory. An absolute cross-laboratory response threshold would be unjustified. See the [matched inputs](../../datasets/pharmacotyping.md) and [precision calculation](../../datasets/precision.md). The exact score × external cohort × endpoint is the novelty question. Another group using those same data for the same claim would turn this into a replication. [Independent cohort](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29857-6) · [GSE194249](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE194249) · [Focused prior-art search](../../literature/pharmacotyping-novelty.md).

**Validation and therapeutic consequence.** Reproduce the original scoring on development data before locking the external score. A single held-out donor-level rank association, interval, specificity controls and sensitivity to pretreatment and proliferation can determine whether the score deserves further investigation. Calibration in a common absolute concentration scale requires comparable curves. A useful laboratory ranking would motivate a blinded assay replication by another laboratory and subsequently a prospective study testing a treatment interaction and patient outcomes. It cannot select a regimen for an individual.

### B. Does the known CDS1–CDS2 relationship transport within pancreatic ductal organoids?

**Known.** CDP-diacylglycerol synthase 1 (CDS1) and CDS2 are paralogues. Two 2025 groups independently reported this interaction. Guo and colleagues directly tested it in pancreatic cancer models. Broad and Sanger have already been used for separate source analyses. The 2026 Broad deposit includes the same expression–dependency pair across 146 next-generation models. [Guo](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2025.116191) · [Arnoldus](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02221-2) · [Chan](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02222-1) · [Broad v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29472362.v1).

**Unresolved question and feasibility.** Within one histology, can the relationship reproduce across donor-independent organoid cohorts? Broad supplies 23 relevant pancreatic three-dimensional model IDs before pathology review. The Sanger validation ceiling is four donors under an inclusive diagnosis, one explicitly ductal. A second paper already evaluates cross-platform transfer for 20 other targets. A sufficiently precise confirmatory answer from these two sources is infeasible. [Source-table audit](../../datasets/feasibility.md) · [Fırat and Kılıçaslan](https://doi.org/10.3390/biology15191782).

**Next verification and therapeutic consequence.** A new donor-disjoint, histologically adjudicated experimental dataset with both target fitness and baseline expression could reopen the question. A generalisable result would still require independent functional perturbation, orthogonal rescue, comparison with non-malignant tissues and an appropriate therapeutic window. The current verdict concerns data adequacy.

### C. Can published resistance markers transfer across inhibitors and treatment settings?

**Known.** RAS (rat sarcoma) inhibition has randomised clinical evidence in previously treated metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma. Functional screens across inhibitors and alleles and newer reports of escape mechanisms already exist. Genetic dependency in untreated lines is a different phenotype from acquired resistance after drug exposure. [RASolute 302](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605555) · [Modifier screens](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41248420/) · [November primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43076691/).

**Unresolved question and data.** Does a published, frozen resistance marker reproduce in a second drug-exposed PDAC cohort after conditioning on the inhibitor, allele, exposure and baseline state? Public screens and longitudinal studies are leads. Usable matched baseline/progression samples and comparable exposures have not been shown here. Verify donor/treatment pairing, prior drug use and longitudinal censoring. Do not promote cross-sectional genotype associations into evidence of resistance.

**Validation and consequence.** Reproduce one published inhibitor-specific contrast and seek an independently exposed cohort. If supported, an external partner would need to test the temporal mechanism and intervention in appropriate preclinical models. Clinical subgroup or combination benefit needs its own comparative study. [Source-specific evidence](../../literature/dependencies.md).

### D. Do stromal or immune signals survive adjustment for tissue composition?

**Known.** Stromal enrichment was insufficient to establish survival benefit in the hyaluronan-selected phase 3 HALO trial. Clinical immune results depend on treatment and setting. Bulk expression, cell-state abundance and conditional response need separate endpoints. [HALO](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.20.00590) · [PRINCE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-01829-9) · [Bai 2026](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01393).

**Question and data.** A fixed cell-state marker could be tested across tumour expression and proteomics with purity, histology and batch prespecified. The Cancer Genome Atlas and Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium have curated PDAC tissues. The endpoint available now is molecular abundance. An immune-treatment interaction also requires independent treated and comparison groups with outcomes, currently unverified.

**Validation and consequence.** A donor-level single-cell or spatial analysis, paired with independently measured protein, could falsify a tumour-cell assignment. A future partner would need spatial or functional tissue confirmation and an appropriate clinical comparison before inferring response. [Inventory](../../datasets/inventory.md).

### E. Do published drug-repurposing or combination findings survive assay change?

**Known.** Screening and combination studies are extensive. Randomised metformin and hydroxychloroquine studies qualify broad positive claims from retrospective or preclinical data. [Metformin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26067687/) · [Hydroxychloroquine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31120501/) · [Organoid biobank and statin combination](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2025.07.008).

**Question and data.** A published single-agent effect could be audited between the PRISM repurposing and Genomics of Drug Sensitivity in Cancer screens after aligning compound identities, exposure and cell-line origin. Shared models permit technical reproducibility. Single-agent tables cannot test synergy. No independently measured common dose-by-dose combination grid has been established here.

**Validation and consequence.** Require same-vehicle and single-agent controls, a preregistered interaction definition and a second laboratory for any combination claim. External biochemical, model and exposure validation would precede therapeutic development. Defer until a concrete compound and a matching experimental dataset can be identified with sufficient precision. [Inventory](../../datasets/inventory.md).

### F. When does an exact RNA folding result transfer to more realistic endpoints?

**Known and question.** The programme's formal theorem, the 2025 base-pair theorem, the 2026 stacked-pair theorem and the 2026 ensemble work address different claims. A fresh build checks a fixed-family result. A public paired experiment already compares Turner outcomes. A narrower contribution could quantify how target duplication, ties and the definition of success affect one published result. Reproduction is ready. Originality of a robustness result remains conditional. [Scientific audit](../PCR-005/audit.md) · [Existing asset and proof register](../PCR-005/assets.md).

**Validation and consequence.** Re-tabulate one archived experiment independently under its original endpoint, then predefine a distinct-structure sensitivity analysis and confidence intervals. More realistic thermodynamics or a partition-function endpoint need dedicated computation and validation. A biological inference would require structural measurement, intracellular function, delivery and activity testing. No PDAC therapeutic target or payload follows from the current methods result.
