# Programme risk register

Status at 27 November 2026. Likelihood and impact below are qualitative judgements. They refer to the proposed studies and are not measured probabilities. The triggers govern research decisions.

| Risk and priority | Evidence or trigger | Mitigation, owner and remaining verification |
|---|---|---|
| False novelty, high | A published supplementary table already gives the CDS1–CDS2 next-generation result. A peer-reviewed 2026 paper explicitly treats stacked pairs. | Work coordinator keeps an exact prior-art ledger, searches forward citations and supplements, and requests an independent novelty challenge before Gate 1. Retire a duplicate. [PDAC audit](../datasets/feasibility.md) · [RNA audit](PCR-005/audit.md) |
| Imprecision or misleading negative finding, high | Four external pancreatic organoid donors with measured knockout and expression, one explicitly ductal. | Statistician publishes the attainable interval or test resolution and screens candidate cohorts before viewing outcomes. Descriptive estimates are admissible. A failed significance test cannot show equivalence. [Aggregate receipt](../datasets/organoid-feasibility.json) |
| Donor leakage and pseudoreplication, high | In the Broad metadata 60 relevant traditional screens belong to 44 distinct models. A donor may have multiple lesions, cultures, releases or assay conditions. Synthetic RNA targets also repeat. | Codex must audit donor/model aliases, source screens and identical structures, group before splitting and bootstrap at the independent unit. Disclose unresolved aliases, subgroup bounds and cohort conditioning. [Feasibility](../datasets/feasibility.md) · [RNA audit](PCR-005/audit.md) |
| Differential ascertainment, high | Failed organoid establishment, treated versus untreated tissue, biopsy origin and growth rate can condition drug sensitivity estimates. Resected cohorts differ from metastatic disease. | Record all attempts and missingness where provided, pretreatment status and medium. Contrast estimable cohort with source population. No inference to routine care from selected organoids. [Clinical evidence](../literature/clinical.md) |
| Endpoint or scale substitution, high | Gene knockout, expression, drug dose–response, inferred dependency, tumour response and survival answer different questions. | Align drug, exposure duration, dose integration, sign and model. Separate descriptive tissue triangulation. Disallow combination benefit attributed to a single agent without suitable controls. [Inventory](../datasets/inventory.md) |
| Analysis flexibility and leakage, high | Feature selection, gene mapping or assay harmonisation can absorb validation outcomes. Millions of possible pairs create multiplicity. | Freeze one score, unit, endpoint, controls, formula, exclusion rules and minimum useful precision before opening validation responses. Use donor-blocked permutations and intervals, false-discovery correction for any prespecified secondary family. Route changes through an amendment. |
| Broken reproducibility or rights, high | Different source terms, an upstream code repository without a licence, integrated matrices and rolling download links. | Owner checks rights, Codex records immutable sources, hashes, dependencies and commands. Keep identifiers and aggregates where permitted. A missing licence sustains a citation-only decision for source transfer. [Licensing](licensing-audit.md) |
| False theoretical or biological generalisation, high | A proof holds under a frozen stacking-only model. Experimental thermodynamic and cellular context add variables. | Independent reviewer checks quantifiers, competitor folds, ties, parameter orientation and units. Keep full-energy calculations and ensemble outcomes empirical. No claim about cellular function or options for patients. [Asset register](PCR-005/assets.md) |
| Bias in evidence retrieval, moderate to high | Indexed preprints and final publications overlap, retrieval engines disagree, abstracts omit negative or supplementary results. | Save exact source queries and identifiers, screen linked versions, use primary supplements. Mask reviewer decisions until adjudication for a formal review. Refresh close to external submission. [Search record](../literature/search/) |
| Privacy and premature external publication, high impact | Public sources can mix open matrices and controlled genomic/clinical records. A draft pull request is visible in the public repository. | Use only ethically permitted public data, store no identifying information in Git, follow repository ethics, and keep any external submission, clinical collaboration or contact subject to explicit owner approval. [Ethics](../RESEARCH_ETHICS.md) |

No experimental or clinical safety decision follows from this register. The next reviewer should focus on the denominator, the transferability of a frozen score and the origin of the comparison cohorts.
