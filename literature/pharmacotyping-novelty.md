# Can the published gemcitabine signature predict response in another pancreatic organoid collection?

**Provisional judgement, 27 November 2026.** A specific replication question merits a feasibility check: does the fixed Tiriac gemcitabine-sensitivity signature rank baseline, donor-distinct pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) organoids inversely to their measured single-agent gemcitabine area under the dose–response curve (AUC) in the Shi collection? Both studies exist. The score itself is established; its external clinical associations are published. The **exact score–cohort–outcome combination** was absent from the screened records. A reproducible external estimate could support or undermine transportability. It would not establish patient response, optimal treatment or clinical utility.

## Files and a falsifiable next step

Tiriac Table S5 version 1, Figshare file 39996292, carries Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 metadata. Its `GEM profile` sheet has 225 unique numeric gene–rho pairs. Exactly **130** are negative, all below −0.38. Mean those genes' standardised expressions with equal weight as specified by the article. The rho column selects features; it is not the set of prediction coefficients. Rebuilding the signature by selecting genes on Shi AUCs would invalidate the proposed test. If article and supplement do not resolve expression transformation or z-score reference population, label the chosen implementation and test it against published training displays before locking it. [Original methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6125219/), [source file](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/39996292)

Shi's outcome sheet contains a normalised AUC for each compound and organoid. Positive per-drug rescaling leaves within-drug rank order unchanged. The external ground truth is laboratory drug response; distinguish from donor survival and response on combination chemotherapy. The paper's human raw-sequencing statement specifies non-commercial consent and a materials transfer agreement. Examine permissions for the **specific processed files** before use. Public availability is not a blanket reuse grant; no raw profiles, gene vectors or individual-level outcomes are reproduced here.

Before linking outcomes, verify assay-replicate collapse, donor independence, histology and the full alias map for the 130 features; record unmapped/zero-variance features before scoring. Register an external rank correlation, donor-level uncertainty interval, permutation null and sensitivity to subtype, growth, platform, medium and treatment history subject to metadata sufficiency. Reproduce the source scoring direction using original training material first. A near-zero, wrong-direction or imprecise association constrains transfer. A positive association would require a further independent cohort to assess robustness, then specialist-led experimental work before drawing a treatment inference. **Confidence:** high in the score/endpoint and already-published comparator distinctions, moderate in the provisional novelty assessment, unresolved for prospective clinical performance.
