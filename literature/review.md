# Initial treatment evidence review, 27 November 2026

**Paused for owner review.** Seven thematic searches retrieved 19,138 distinct source identifiers from PubMed and Europe PubMed Central. The four overlapping, fully dispositioned screening sets contain 453 distinct source identifiers. These include 225 phase III-indexed records from the disease-only search. Individual decisions include unresolved cases and repeat reports. The broad corpus has not undergone independent full-text screening. There is no pooled treatment effect or clinical recommendation.

| Evidence stream | Findings and research decision |
|---|---|
| Genetic dependencies, synthetic lethality, KRAS (Kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene homolog) and treatment resistance | Pancreatic and cross-lineage experiments already established the CDS1–CDS2 interaction. Only four donors in the external pancreatic organoid resource have both expression and screened fitness. The data cannot support the proposed confirmatory study. [Dependency synthesis](dependencies.md) · [Source-table verification](../datasets/feasibility.md) |
| Microenvironment, immune evasion, repurposing, combination therapies and RNA (ribonucleic acid) therapeutics | Comparative studies include survival benefits in specific molecular/clinical settings, absent benefit despite responses, and an unfavourable survival outcome despite target engagement. Early vaccine and delivery studies address different endpoints. [Clinical evidence and limitations](clinical.md) · [Phase III screen](search/phase3/README.md) |
| Computational therapeutic discovery | A fixed published drug-response score could be challenged in 38 external ductal organoids. The public expression matrix represents 123 of 130 required genes even after a nomenclature audit. The exact score cannot be tested from this matrix alone. A full permitted matrix, or separate prespecification and owner approval of an altered score, is required. [Novelty assessment](pharmacotyping-novelty.md) · [Masked data audit](../datasets/pharmacotyping.md) · [Precision study](../datasets/precision.md) |
| RNA inverse folding | A clean proof rebuild confirms the fixed stacking-only result for one three-helix family. The 2026 follow-up already treats stacked-pair design and tests Turner predictions. The archived synthetic benchmark supports independent paired reproduction. Direct therapeutic relevance is unestablished. [RNA audit](../research/PCR-005/audit.md) · [Asset register](../research/PCR-005/assets.md) |

The [search protocol, dated queries, identifier exports and study decisions](search/README.md) distinguish preprints, trial reports, contexts and exclusions. The [clinical supplement](clinical-native-screen.tsv) and [pharmacotyping screen](pharmacotyping-search.tsv) overlap other streams; totals cannot be added as independent studies. Observational data, preclinical perturbations and clinical outcomes are appraised separately in the linked reports. Further independent eligibility adjudication and structured risk-of-bias assessment are outstanding.
