# PCR-001 | Genetic dependence, acquired resistance and novelty in pancreatic cancer

**Decision on 27 November 2026.** The link between low expression of CDP-diacylglycerol synthase 1 (CDS1) and dependence on its paralogue CDS2 has experimental support in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) cells. Its existence, its association with cell state and its detection in pooled three-dimensional cancer models are established prior art. A narrower contribution could estimate how precisely that relation transfers to independently screened PDAC organoids. The Sanger reference resource contains a maximum of **four** pancreatic donors with both expression and perturbation data. It cannot by itself provide persuasive confirmation of an organoid biomarker. A documented limit to identifiability is a valid deliverable.

KRAS denotes Kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene homolog. CRISPR denotes clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic repeats. EMT denotes epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition.

## Resistance and therapeutic context

* In 2017, Muzumdar et al. found that some human and mouse PDAC cells could survive KRAS loss. Biancur et al. in 2020 found shared metabolic dependencies in two- and three-dimensional culture and showed how nutrient sharing can conceal a dependency. These experiments explain why conventional screens can produce both plausible hits and false reassurance. [Muzumdar](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00942-5), [Biancur](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.10.018)
* Singhal et al. observed acutely resistant classical-state cells under KRAS inhibition in preclinical models. Laise et al. reported developmental cell states and signalling substates with experimentally supported dependencies. A descriptive expression score in a bulk tumour blends tumour and stromal cells, and the pre-treatment and resistant states need separate endpoints. [Singhal 2024](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-24-0740), [Laise, 26 Aug 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02714-8)
* Long et al. screened six inhibitors across cell lines representing four KRAS variants and found variant- and context-specific responses. Separate November 2026 papers found changes in Src-family kinase signalling and ferroptosis in resistant models. Their interventions, model systems and timing differ. A generic screen for KRAS resistance or proposal to combine inhibitors would repeat published work. [Long, Feb 2026 issue](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-1835), [Ting, 24 Nov 2026](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-26-0691), [phosphoproteomics, 24 Nov 2026](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-26-0777), [ferroptosis, 3 Nov 2026](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-26-1490)
* Citation chasing from the November PubMed commentary retrieved a primary study by Zhang et al. in a collection of 242 patient-derived organoids with matched tissue. It linked lower innate immune signalling to resistance in a subset of G12D-mutant models. Total bank size does not give the denominator for any specific drug assay. Its endpoint is inhibitor sensitivity, so it cannot substitute for CDS2 loss-of-function measurements. [Cancer Cell, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2026.10.003)
* A July 2026 **bioRxiv preprint record** proposed separate baseline focal adhesion kinase and induced receptor hypotheses. Its cell-line pharmacological comparison had five lines and uncertainty intervals spanning zero for the modest response. The report supplied no direct evidence that a proposed combination improves patient outcomes. [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.09.737590)
* The phase III RASolute 302 trial randomised 500 previously treated patients with metastatic pancreatic cancer to daraxonrasib or chemotherapy. Its randomised outcomes, with primary endpoints in the RAS G12 subgroup, establish direct clinical relevance of the RAS pathway in this setting. They do not test CDS1, CDS2 or any proposed co-inhibitor. [Randomised trial, 31 May 2026](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605555), [trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320), [US regulatory assessment](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-daraxonrasib-metastatic-pancreatic-adenocarcinoma)

## Gap verdict and falsification

**Known:** the underlying interaction has been experimentally tested in PDAC and separately seen with pooled next-generation models. **Unresolved:** within authenticated PDAC organoids, what effect can be estimated for a fixed CDS1-expression/CDS2-gene-effect endpoint across donor-disjoint collections after excluding shared specimens and repeated assay releases? **Closest overlap:** Guo, Neiswender and Fırat above. Confidence is high in the duplicated broad claims and moderate that the exact disease-specific precision audit is missing. Evaluate source tables and later citing publications again before registering originality.

**Measured feasibility constraint:** with four independent pancreatic donors, even perfect rank ordering without ties has an exact two-sided permutation probability of 2/24, about 0.083. Histological filtering can reduce this further. This arithmetic is not a biological test result. Predefine a scientifically meaningful precision target and check attainable intervals without inspecting expression–fitness associations. If available data cannot distinguish relevant effect sizes, record the feasibility failure and seek an independent public resource. Do not replace missing CDS2 outcomes with RNA expression, switch drugs, change thresholds, or pool other cancers after seeing the denominator.

A self-hosted October 2026 report also claims to compare KRAS dependency in pancreatic two- and three-dimensional cultures using the Neiswender data. The accessible webpage did not mention CDS2. Treat it as grey literature for the novelty search. Its analyses were not independently reproduced here. [Self-hosted report](https://www.zannixlabs.com/research/depmap-pancreatic-dependencies)

**Possible downstream value (judgement):** a reliable transport estimate could guide selection of preclinical models for evaluating a biomarker, and failure to transport would constrain an often-assumed extrapolation. Independent specialist experiments would still need disease-confirmed organoids, orthogonal perturbation and rescue, normal pancreatic controls and eventual selective pharmacology addressing exposure and normal-tissue toxicity. No therapeutic window, treatment choice or clinical effect has been demonstrated by this audit.
