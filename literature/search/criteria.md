# Index search and screening criteria

Cut-off 27 November 2026 (inclusive). Two native bibliographic indexes were queried without a language, access or species filter. PubMed title/abstract and date of publication fields differ from Europe PubMed Central (Europe PMC) title/abstract and first-publication-date fields. Records registered late, ahead of print, dated only to a year, or indexed as patents or preprints may differ between sources. Counts describe what the indexes returned on retrieval. A later rerun with the same date cut-off can change.

The seven broad arms combine the pancreatic disease clause with terms for dependencies, KRAS (Kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene homolog), resistance, microenvironment, drug combinations or repurposing, ribonucleic acid therapeutics and computational target discovery. Their purpose is a reproducible candidate universe. Other cancer types can match via comparisons, and our deliberately sensitive treatment terms also retrieve diagnosis or basic biology. The broad census alone cannot demonstrate completeness, novelty or absence of an association. Disease synonyms miss associations made only in supplementary material or via a named model. Indexed headings, trial-registry records, linked reports, backwards/forwards citations and an information specialist’s search are complementary. The two narrative evidence maps use their separately documented targeted queries.

## Focused novelty screen

Question: Does prior work already report the lipid-synthesis paralogues CDS1 and CDS2 in pancreatic cancer, their relationship to cancer dependency, or experimental evidence relevant to that relationship? Include original mammalian cancer studies with relevant dependency evidence, also mechanistic investigations of the same paralogues in cancer when they test a distinct proposition. Retain secondary evidence or preprint versions separately. Exclude other genes with the same acronym, other diseases, studies of normal physiology without relevant cancer evidence, patent documents and nonempirical topical mentions. Include all indexed dates through cut-off. Examine each returned record; use the abstract or linked primary article for ambiguous titles. State the reason for every decision. A result of a different cancer phenotype does not count as replication. PubMed identifiers and Europe PMC MED identifiers identify one report. Other source identifiers are retained and linked to a journal version when verifiable.

The first query was deliberately narrow. A known companion paper (PubMed 40615675) was missed. The amended query adds cancer, cancers, tumor, tumour, carcinoma, mesenchymal and “synthetic lethal” to the association clause. It is a sensitivity amendment after reviewing known seeds. Findings do not constitute a prospective test of sensitivity. Record each version, including negative and off-topic hits.

## Dated surveillance screen

To screen material at the upper boundary instead of assuming a cut-off is current, take the union of the seven theme vocabularies and query 1–27 November 2026. Screen all results from three separately labelled filters: indexed randomised controlled trials, indexed systematic reviews or meta-analyses, and indexed preprints. Date is the index-specific publication field above. A separate longer preprint census starts 1 January 2025. The short-window filters rely on indexer publication types; they miss newly published studies whose types are pending. They also miss studies indexed online in an earlier month.

Include PDAC (pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma) treatment, experimental response, dependency, computational target studies or treatment-associated microenvironment work. Include mixed-tumour studies only when interpretable pancreatic results can be distinguished; flag uncertain histology. Mark diagnostically or etiologically focused studies, pancreatic metastases of a different primary, risk prediction, noninterventional prognosis alone, surgery alone, methods without pancreatic results and records out of the actual publication window separately. Link duplicate posted and journal versions without double counting. Report study design and whether a result was peer reviewed. This is a single-reviewer screen, with ambiguous classifications flagged for adjudication. Record availability of same-donor or same-model data as a distinct validation issue.

## Historical high-level evidence sensitivity search

For all dates in the seven-theme corpus, screen every record indexed as either a phase III clinical trial or a systematic review. Include PDAC treatment trials, trial secondary analyses pertinent to therapeutic response, and systematic reviews of treatment effects, resistance, target biology or qualifying preclinical treatment strategies. Mark secondary analyses, mixed histologies and animal or cell-line evidence separately. Exclude protocols without results, treatment-independent prognostic or diagnostic studies, pancreatic metastases of other primaries, aetiology/prevention-only work, supportive care without tumour-related therapeutic outcomes, and reviews without separable pancreatic results. Inspect titles and available abstracts for every record, and check full-text pivotal reports already extracted in the clinical section. Historical counts and treatment selection depend on title/abstract terms and indexing. Registry searches and reference checking can identify landmark trials missed by the themed vocabulary. This supplementary slice was added after the initial evidence map to increase coverage of higher-level studies.
