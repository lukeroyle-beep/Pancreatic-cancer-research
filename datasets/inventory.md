# Public dataset inventory for pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma

Assessed 27 November 2026. These are candidate releases and study freezes for pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Counts below describe the unit the provider or paper counted. A catalogue entry, assayed model, replicate, donor and person with a measured clinical endpoint are different denominators. Counts are published unless explicitly labelled as file recounts. This inventory inspected release metadata, selected public catalogues, fitted-response workbook identifiers and public reports. It did not download patient data, certify redistribution rights or independently reproduce assay results.

## Which records could support independent verification?

| Question | Discovery and verification records | What the design can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Does a known genetic effect reproduce across laboratories on the *same* lines? | Dempster et al. assembled **147 shared cell lines and 16,733 shared genes**, with institute-specific measurements from Broad 19Q1 and Sanger. The historical archive contains source-labelled data. The later integrated matrix combined **908** different lines and 17,486 genes. [Matched study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13805-y) · [Paired deposit v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7970993.v1) · [Integrated study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-21898-7) | Estimate paired between-laboratory agreement; stratify by histology after resolving identities. Shared lines support technical/assay reproducibility. Evidence for biological generalisation requires different donors/models. A model trained on the integrated matrix has already seen Sanger measurements. [Sanger file terms](https://www.sanger.ac.uk/tool/project-score-database/) |
| Does an effect seen in cell lines also occur in organoids from other donors? | Broad next-generation study, “NextGen Model Manuscript 2026”, plus deposited analytical files. Sanger independently reports **256 established tumour organoids and 162 screened** across five cancers; Figure 1 and Supplementary Tables enumerate models and assay availability. Match source, donor, provenance and culture explicitly, since both initiatives involve shared resources. [Broad analytical files](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29472362) · [Sanger paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10830-y) · [Sanger analytical files](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28339340) | The denominator for a target and genotype can be much smaller than the biobank. Their separate Tiriac projection cohort lacked measured dependencies. This limits the precision of pancreatic-only estimates; it does not count all pancreatic samples in every release. [Publication, methods and limitations](https://doi.org/10.3390/biology15191782) · [Frozen replication package v1.0.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22472622) |
| Can a computationally identified drug relationship be checked in a separate experimental system? | A 2022 study established 31 genetically distinct pancreatic organoid lines and screened a library of 1,172 approved compounds. Distinguish biobank size from the number actually tested per drug in each stage; audit the tested-subset table, experiments and supplement licence before use. GSE169321 can independently assess expression changes in paired material. ## Before opening the feasibility gate

1. Build a metadata-only manifest. Record study accession/DOI (digital object identifier), version, date retrieved, exact file, release-specific terms and attribution, units, original identifiers, file size, publisher checksum and a local Secure Hash Algorithm 256-bit (SHA-256) checksum. A publication cohort or rolling accession is an incomplete download pin. Do not put restricted files or identity mappings in the repository.
2. Produce an attrition table starting with all models and ending with unique, histology-confirmed PDAC donors/models by assay, gene, allele, study, source laboratory and culture type. Separate unknown histology or genotype from confirmed controls. Report how many identifiers were ambiguous, excluded or multiply represented. For compound assays, also count by drug and effective dose range. Refuse a fuzzy name match unless a source cross-reference resolves it.
3. Publish an aggregate overlap matrix for exact models, related derivatives, shared donors, screens and publications. Trace integration and normalisation provenance, including pooled or cross-site correction. Assign each unit to discovery, paired assay check, genuinely held-out validation or descriptive context before inspecting outcomes. Fit transformations and thresholds in discovery only.
4. Calculate attainable precision from those denominators and the declared independent unit. Where the prespecified contrast is unidentifiable, detail the missing measurement or overlap. Use data-access scripts or instructions and permitted aggregate results; a right to read a third-party file does not by itself grant a right to republish it with an open-source licence.

## Acquisition and verification limits

High confidence in the labelled paper/provider denominators, cohort definitions, structural entry versions and the distinction between assay replication and held-out biology. Moderate confidence in practical feasibility pending file-level checks. Access verification and JavaScript-dependent pages limited inspection of some licence fields and files. The PRISM version 4 record and secondary catalogue were separately verified. The separate [gene-dependency](feasibility.md) and [gemcitabine](pharmacotyping.md) audits give directly measured denominators for specific questions. Other drug-wise complete-case counts and cross-study donor overlap require file-level verification. No unrestricted discovery experiment follows from this inventory alone.
