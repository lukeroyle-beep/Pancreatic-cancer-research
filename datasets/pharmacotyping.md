# Feasibility of a frozen gemcitabine signature across organoid cohorts

**27 November 2026.** Does Tiriac et al.’s published expression score rank independently measured single-agent gemcitabine sensitivity in ductal organoids from a separate laboratory? The score and its original clinical associations are published. A possible contribution is a prespecified external test of rank performance and failure modes. We checked five source files and a gene nomenclature archive, their identifiers, gene labels and the presence of numeric measurements. We did not calculate or inspect a score–response association.

## Verified model and feature joins

We matched exact, case-sensitive model identifiers across the numeric GEM row of Shi Supplementary Data 8, the clinical annotations in Data 1 and the header of the processed expression matrix. All 39 screened identifiers matched all three files. The histology column identified **38 pancreatic ductal adenocarcinomas** and one invasive intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm. All 38 eligible records report one sample per patient. Of these, 36 were primary specimens and two metastatic; 37 had no recorded neoadjuvant therapy and one did. This establishes within-study sample accounting, subject to the accuracy of the source annotations. It does not prove donor independence between institutions.

In Tiriac S4A, 66 unique model IDs have a numeric gemcitabine entry. S5A has 225 numeric gene–response correlations. Applying the paper’s sensitivity direction and threshold (rho below −0.38) retains 130 gene symbols. **123/130** appear exactly once in the Shi expression rows; seven have no exact symbol match. The HUGO (Human Genome Organisation) Gene Nomenclature Committee (HGNC) quarterly archive of 2 October 2026 lists these seven as approved symbols. None of their previous or alternative symbols occurs in the 15,609 unique expression rows. Thus the directly recoverable coverage remains **123/130 (94.6%)**. The absent symbols are CENPX, CNOT9, ESS2, MAIP1, NSD2, RCC1L and SELENOI. A score built from the 123 available genes is a modified score requiring its own interpretation. [Nomenclature archive and fields](https://www.genenames.org/download/) Tiriac’s two Figshare files carry Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 in their version metadata. [S4 metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22533049/versions/1) · [S5 metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22533046/versions/1) · [Shi supplementary file index](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/studies/S-EPMC9023604)

Reproduce the counts from the repository root with Python 3.12.14 and openpyxl 3.1.5. The checker downloads public files to a caller-specified local directory, verifies six SHA-256 (Secure Hash Algorithm 256-bit) digests, and prints aggregate metadata. It does not use the magnitude or ranking of measured drug effects. The sampling design, unverified cross-site donor overlap and analysis conventions still require review.

**Why use Shi before Driehuis?** Driehuis et al. measured single-agent gemcitabine, including four clinical comparisons, and screened 24 organoids with a 76-drug panel. The genomic and transcriptomic deposit is at the European Genome-phenome Archive, EGAS00001003369. Public processed organoid-level expression suitable for this test was not verified. Their single-agent half-maximal inhibitory concentrations and combination excess-area measurements have different interpretations. Shi provides public processed expression, individual organoid identifiers and a gemcitabine-only area endpoint. [Driehuis et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6936689/) · [Archive](https://ega-archive.org/studies/EGAS00001003369)

**Prior art and independence.** Tiriac's original study derived and tested its signature, including in patients who received gemcitabine. A separate GemPred study combined primary cultures and organoids to develop a gemcitabine signature. Shi mapped chromatin accessibility against drug response. **Gate decision.** Proceed to independent reference score reconstruction and protocol review. Record release-specific rights for public reporting. Shi reports non-commercial consent and a materials transfer agreement for raw sequencing data. Check the intended use of public processed files against consent, archive and supplement terms. Raw sequence is unnecessary. Confirm a unique gemcitabine gene list, directions and scoring rule, then reproduce the published score to the extent permitted. Verify the file audit independently and assess attainable precision at 38 eligible donors. An illustrative Fisher-transformed correlation interval for an observed correlation of zero would span approximately −0.32 to +0.32 at n=38 (half-width on the transformed scale 1.96/√35, independent bivariate-normal observations). This is a planning approximation for Pearson correlation, not an interval measured here or a power calculation for the proposed rank test. Specify an informative performance margin and design precision for the rank estimand before evaluating outcomes. For the original full score, obtain a permission-compatible expression source that measures the seven missing genes or stop. A reduced-score study would need an explicitly new estimand, reference-side leave-seven-out sensitivity analysis, and a scoring rule frozen before external evaluation. Establish the training-standardisation reference or reproduce the published within-cohort rule from the source methods. The exemplars named in the published article were already visible; disclose this and rerun a sensitivity analysis excluding them.

For later preregistration, propose one signed rank correlation between the frozen score and measured sensitivity (negative normalised AUC), a donor-level uncertainty interval and a permutation null. Prespecify sensitivity to baseline proliferation and subtype. Document whether gene standardisation is within cohort or anchored to a reference. Keep all score choices independent of external outcomes. Inconclusive or reversed association is a reportable result. Clinical treatment benefit remains a separate question.

**Confidence.** High that the same single agent and measured organoid response occur in both studies, with 38 exact eligible Shanghai joins. Moderate that a sufficiently complete, rights-compatible donor-level replication is feasible. The exact reference-cohort expression–response intersection, availability of the seven missing inputs, cross-study independence, score reconstruction, attainable precision and novelty require verification.
